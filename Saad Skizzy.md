### Video editing

TikTok · short-form

I cut vertical videos where the picture, the sound and the cuts land together. I work on pacing, transitions and effects that fit the song, so an edit feels smooth to watch on repeat.

- Transitions

### Discord bots

Commands · moderation · data

I build bots that run a community without extra effort: slash commands, moderation tools, logging and levels, with member data saved in a database so nothing is lost on a restart.

Sites · interfaces · motion
